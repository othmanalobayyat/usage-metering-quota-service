Quota service: example client

Each run creates a new account on a custom plan that allows 200 units a month and at most 50 per request, with two users: Alice on Basic (20 units per request) and Bob on Premium (100 per request). Alice uses 10 units, then asks for 25, which is over her per-request limit. She moves to Premium and asks for the same 25 again. Bob then uses up the rest of the account's monthly allowance, and his next request is refused. Every call to the quota service goes through this page's own server.
